Calculation assumptions

Cumulative FD: quarterly compounding using (1 + annual rate / 400) raised to four times the tenure in years. For partial quarters, a fractional-quarter mathematical approximation is used. Monthly payout FD: illustrative monthly-discounted interest calculated using the quarterly equivalent rate, i.e. principal × [(1 + annual rate / 400)^(1/3) − 1]; quarterly payout: principal × annual rate / 400. These payout conventions are estimates and actual bank disbursements may vary due to product rules, day counts, rounding and payment dates.

SCSS: fixed 5-year term, quarterly simple-interest payments, no cumulative or monthly option, and no interest-on-interest. The default 8.20% is an editable example, not a verified currently notified rate. SCSS has statutory eligibility and deposit limits; the calculator uses a ₹30 lakh illustrative maximum and does not determine eligibility. Tax, TDS, premature withdrawal, extensions and penalties are excluded.
